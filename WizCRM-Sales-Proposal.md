## Executive summary

**WizCRM** is an **AI sales operating system** built for sales and field teams who need results—not another empty CRM that reps avoid updating.

Your team talks to prospects, meets customers, and closes deals. WizCRM’s AI captures that work, structures your pipeline, recommends what to do next, drafts follow-ups, and gives managers clear visibility—while keeping **humans in control**: you approve messages, stage changes, and important updates before they go live.

This proposal outlines WizCRM’s modular capabilities, three commercial plans (**Lite**, **Pro**, **Enterprise**), and how WIZAG can help you move from pilot to full rollout.

**Recommended positioning:** Do not buy “CRM with AI features.” Buy **AI that does the CRM work**—so reps sell more and managers see reality faster.

## Product overview

WizCRM is delivered as one product brand with tiered capability. All plans share the same core philosophy: **smallest possible input from the user**, **approve before send** for customer-facing actions, **AI audit** of suggestions vs approvals, and **graceful fallback** when AI or connectivity is unavailable (manual notes and basic fields always work).

**Platforms:** Mobile-first experience (Android priority for field and post-call workflows); full web + mobile on **Pro** and **Enterprise**.

**Lead lifecycle:** Standard pipeline stages—New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost—with AI-suggested transitions and full audit trail on every change.
