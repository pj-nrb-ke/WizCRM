# WizCRM — Product features by plan

Reps focus on selling. AI captures activity, updates the CRM, recommends next actions, drafts follow-ups, and gives managers visibility—with **your approval** before messages and important changes go out.

## WizCRM Lite

*Internal pilot — prove value with your own sales teams in about one week.*

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Quick lead capture** | Add a lead in seconds: name plus phone or email; company optional. |
| **Business card scan** | Photograph a card; AI extracts name, company, phone, and email for you to confirm. |
| **Duplicate protection** | Warns if phone or email already exists before you create a duplicate. |
| **AI Sales Desk (basic)** | Opens to a short “do today” list: priority leads and follow-ups. |
| **AI lead summary** | Plain-language summary of what is happening with each lead. |
| **AI next action** | One clear recommendation: call, follow up, send info, etc. |
| **Post-call logging** | After a call, attach it to the right lead; speak or type a rough note; AI writes the CRM entry. |
| **Voice or quick notes** | Short voice note or bullet text converted into a clean timeline entry. |
| **Simple pipeline** | See leads by stage; AI can suggest stage changes—you approve. |
| **Follow-up tasks** | AI suggests reminders; you edit dates and mark done. |
| **Activity timeline** | Calls, notes, and stage changes in one chronological feed. |

**Lite is not for:** multi-company SaaS billing, manager dashboards, quotes, maps, geofence check-in, or ERP.

## WizCRM Pro

*The main product for most customers — full AI sales OS in about one month after Lite.*

*Everything in **Lite**, plus:*

### AI workspace

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Sales Desk (full)** | Hot leads, follow-ups due, stale leads, quote follow-ups, and recent activity in one daily workspace. |
| **Lead priority & risk** | Scores for fit, engagement, urgency, and deal risk so reps know who matters first. |
| **Smarter lead capture** | AI suggests lead source and priority; completeness hints for sales-ready records. |

### AI activity & follow-up

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Call outcomes** | AI suggests interested, follow-up, objection, no answer, not interested. |
| **Meeting intelligence** | Meeting notes turned into decisions, objections, next steps, and tasks. |
| **AI follow-up engine** | Auto-created tasks, smart reminder timing, missed follow-up alerts. |
| **Draft messages** | AI drafts email, WhatsApp, or SMS follow-ups—you review and send. |
| **Objection helpers** | Draft replies for price, timing, competitor, and budget objections. |

### Pipeline & management

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI pipeline coach** | Stage suggestions, stuck-deal warnings, basic win probability, simple forecast. |
| **AI Manager Cockpit** | Daily or weekly plain-English team summary; pipeline snapshot; who needs attention. |
| **Targets & pacing** | Monthly targets per rep; on-track vs behind; forecast vs target in plain language. |
| **Data hygiene** | Flags missing data, duplicates, and dormant leads. |

### Quotes & platform

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Quotations Lite** | Build quotes with line items; AI drafts customer-facing text; track sent/accepted/rejected. |
| **Quote follow-up** | Reminders and draft nudges when quotes go quiet. |
| **Web + mobile** | Full experience in browser and on phone. |
| **Teams & roles** | Sales, manager, and admin roles per company. |
| **Multi-tenant SaaS** | Secure separate workspaces per customer organization. |
| **ScaleGate licensing** | Subscription and plan enforcement via your commercial licensing platform. |
| **Export** | Export leads and activities to CSV for reporting. |

**Pro is not for:** geofence visit proof, live ERP sync, full email inbox sync, or enterprise SSO.
