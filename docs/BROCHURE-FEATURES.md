# WizCRM — Feature List for Brochure Design

**Positioning line:** WizCRM is the AI-first CRM built for African field-sales teams — a CRM that writes itself, prospects for you, manages your reps, and tells you exactly what every deal is worth.

**Audience:** SME sales leaders and field-sales teams (Kenya/East Africa first).

**Format note:** grouped by theme, each with a one-line hook + supporting bullets. Pull whichever sections fit the brochure's page count — the ⭐ items are the strongest headline candidates.

---

## ⭐ 1. AI Visit Capture — "The CRM that writes itself"

The flagship feature. A rep finishes a customer visit, talks for 20–30 seconds, and the CRM does the paperwork.

- Speak naturally about the visit — no forms, no typing
- AI transcribes and drafts a full structured report: outcome, who was met, competitor mentioned, objection raised, next step, suggested due date, clean summary
- Every field is editable before saving — AI drafts, the rep decides
- Turns a 5-minute chore reps skip into a 20-second habit they'll actually do
- Works fully offline — syncs the moment a connection returns

## ⭐ 2. Virtual Sales Manager — "Wanjiru"

An AI persona that runs the daily management rhythm your team currently doesn't have time for.

- Reads the whole pipeline every morning — stale leads, overdue tasks, today's calendar, targets vs. actuals
- Assigns each rep a short, reasoned task list by email + in-app, with a one-line "why" for every item
- Nudges reps who've gone quiet during the day
- Sends the owner one daily brief instead of five people's worth of status-chasing
- Escalates only what genuinely needs a human decision — never a disciplinary tool
- Starts in Draft mode (you approve each morning plan) and graduates to Auto once a clean approval streak is on record

## ⭐ 3. AI-Assisted Prospecting — Lead Generator

Describe your ideal customer once; the engine finds, scores, and enriches matching companies for you.

- Discovers companies from Google Maps data, scoped to your target market
- Scores every prospect 0–85 on industry fit, web presence, and reputation — sorted into A/B/C tiers, fully explainable (no black box)
- Enriches survivors by reading their website — company summary, size, sector, and whether they already run competing software
- Finds the actual decision-makers (procurement, ops, finance, IT, MD/CEO) via a multi-provider waterfall
- **Heat Map** — a live radar of companies showing buying intent right now, scanned across 9 sources (tenders, hiring signals, new company registrations, forums)
- Built-in email sequences with open/click/reply tracking; a reply auto-imports the prospect into your pipeline
- Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA) compliant at every step

## 4. Opportunity Cost Center & Commission Engine

Turn every deal into a real, trackable cost center — not just a pipeline card.

- Every sales opportunity gets its own documents, notes, and tasks — tag uploads as Quotation, Proforma Invoice, Invoice, or LPO
- Live budget vs. spend vs. revenue vs. margin, flagged automatically when a deal goes over budget
- The moment a customer LPO lands, managers get an instant alert — no more waiting to be told a deal is committed
- Automatic sales commission calculation from real invoice totals — forecasted the moment a quote goes out, confirmed the moment the invoice is raised
- Commission becomes collectible in proportion to what the customer has actually paid — a built-in incentive for reps to chase payment, not just close deals
- Org-wide commission liability report broken down by salesperson, plus a running "pending commission" number on every rep's own dashboard

## 5. Core Lead & Pipeline Management

The solid CRM backbone underneath everything else.

- Fully customizable pipeline stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost)
- Duplicate detection on every new lead — never create the same contact twice
- Drag-and-drop Kanban board with rule-enforced stage transitions
- Full activity timeline per lead: calls, emails, meetings, notes, stage history
- Bulk import, bulk reassignment, and bulk stage changes for managers
- Structured Won/Lost outcomes with deal value and loss-reason tracking

## 6. Built for the Field — Mobile App

A native app built for reps on the road, not a shrunk-down website.

- One-tap call, WhatsApp, and Google Maps directions straight from a lead
- Business-card scanning — photograph a card, AI fills in the lead
- GPS-verified, geofenced meeting check-in/check-out — real field accountability
- Document library reps can share to a customer over WhatsApp on the spot
- Fully offline-first — notes, tasks, visit reports, and photos all queue and sync automatically
- Local reminders and push notifications for tasks, meetings, and follow-ups

## 7. Reports Managers Actually Read

Executive-level visibility without exporting a single spreadsheet.

- Role-adaptive dashboard: open, won, stale, overdue, win rate — with drill-down to the underlying leads
- Conversion funnel with time-in-stage analytics
- Rep performance leaderboard and live team workload
- Weighted pipeline forecast and monthly targets/pacing (ahead/on track/behind)
- Data hygiene report — missing contacts, stale leads, uncontacted new leads, duplicates
- One-paragraph AI-written morning brief for the owner

## 8. Team, Admin & White-Label Controls

Run the platform the way your business actually works.

- Role model: reps see their own book, managers see the team, admins configure the org
- Configurable lead sources, loss reasons, stale-lead thresholds, geofence radius
- White-label branding — your logo, your accent color, applied live across the app
- Granular per-role feature permissions
- Commission settings: org-wide on/off, per-salesperson rate overrides, never retroactive

## 9. Security & Compliance

Built with a Kenya-specific privacy story from day one.

- KDPA-classified contact data with a configurable gate/block mode for personal identifiers
- Full data-subject deletion and suppression-list support
- Every AI action is logged and auditable — nothing the AI does is invisible
- Role-based access control, hardened login (lockouts, rate limits), encrypted sessions
