## ⭐ The headline: a CRM that writes itself

A rep finishes a customer visit, opens WizCRM, taps one button, and **just talks for 20 seconds.**

> *"Met the operations manager. They're keen but worried about price. Competitor is X. They want a quote by Thursday."*

By the time they've put the phone down, WizCRM has written the whole visit report:

- ✅ **Outcome** — "Requested quote"
- ✅ **Who they met** — Operations Manager
- ✅ **Competitor** — flagged
- ✅ **Objection** — price
- ✅ **Next step** — send quotation, **due Thursday** (real date, added to their tasks)
- ✅ **A clean summary** in the timeline
- ✅ **A nudge** to advance the deal a stage

Every field is pre-filled and fully editable — the rep glances, tweaks if needed, and saves. **What used to be a five-minute chore is now a twenty-second voice note.**

The result? Your CRM actually gets filled in. Managers finally see what's happening in the field. And reps get their evenings back.

## The AI advantage — woven through the whole day

WizCRM's AI isn't a chatbot in the corner. It shows up exactly where reps need a hand:

| Moment | What AI does for the rep |
|---|---|
| **📇 Meeting someone new** | Snap their business card — WizCRM reads it and creates the lead, no typing |
| **📞 After a call** | Jot a rough note — AI turns it into a clean summary, a follow-up task, and a stage suggestion |
| **🎙 After a visit** | Speak it — AI writes the full report (above) |
| **🤔 "What do I do next?"** | AI recommends the single best next action for each lead, with a reason |
| **☀️ Start of the day** | A prioritised "do this today" list across the rep's whole book |

## Fill your pipeline — automatically

Stop buying stale lead lists. Tell WizCRM your ideal customer — *"construction firms in Nairobi"* — and the **Lead Engine** goes to work:

1. **Discovers** matching companies across the web and business directories
2. **Scores and ranks** them A / B / C on fit, reputation, and how real the business is — so reps chase the best first
3. **Enriches** each one from its website (size, sector, what software they already run)
4. **Finds the decision-makers** — the procurement, finance, ops, and IT leaders who actually sign
5. **De-duplicates** against your existing pipeline and hands reps a clean, ranked list

### 🔥 Know who's ready to buy — right now

The **Heat Map** scans live buying signals across Kenya — government tenders, "looking for a supplier" posts, hiring sprees, new company registrations — and plots them on an interactive map, graded **HOT / WARM**. Your team calls the companies raising their hand *this week*, not next quarter.

### Automated, respectful outreach

Launch multi-step email sequences that **stop the moment a prospect replies** — and when they do, they're pulled straight into your pipeline. Every message includes one-click unsubscribe, and opt-outs are honoured automatically.

## Built for Africa, built for trust

WizCRM is designed for the Kenyan and African SME market from the ground up — not a Silicon Valley product bolted onto local needs.

- 🇰🇪 **KDPA-compliant by design.** WizCRM respects the Kenya Data Protection Act at every step — classifying data, honouring do-not-contact and unsubscribe requests, and supporting data-subject deletion. Prospect responsibly, and prove it.
- 🛡 **Enterprise-grade security.** Encrypted sessions, hardened logins, role-based access, and a complete audit trail of every AI action — nothing the system does is invisible.
- 📶 **Made for the field.** Offline-first, low-bandwidth-friendly, and WhatsApp-native, because that's how business actually gets done here.
- 🤝 **AI you can trust.** Every AI suggestion is transparent, reviewable, and only ever a draft until your team approves it.
