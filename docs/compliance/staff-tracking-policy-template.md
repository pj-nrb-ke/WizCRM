# Staff tracking / field check-in policy (draft — HR/Legal review)

When using WizCRM mobile **meeting check-in**, your location at check-in time may be recorded to verify attendance.

- Check-in is **voluntary** except where your employer policy requires it
- Location is used for attendance proof, not continuous background tracking
- Geofence radius is configured by your organization (see CRM settings)

Employees should raise questions with [HR contact].
